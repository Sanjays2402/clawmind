## Why this was a real fix, not cosmetic

Re-confirmed (grep): `--bg / --card / --border / --muted-fg / --fg-muted /
--bg-elev / --bg-hover / --accent / --accent-fg` are DEFINED NOWHERE in the app -
only `--cm-*` ships in `packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css`. So all 5 pages were
rendering on broken CSS fallbacks: muted copy inherited full --cm-fg (no contrast
hierarchy), cards had no surface fill, "accent" buttons had no brand color, and
destructive actions rendered on raw emerald/amber/red that ignore the palette.
